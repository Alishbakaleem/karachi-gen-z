ABOUT THE PROJECT

Karachi, made easier to explore.

Karachi Gen Z is a student-made guide to discovering cafés, things to do, student resources, and useful spots around Karachi.

Why I built it

Karachi Gen Z started as a way to create a simple, useful guide to the city from a student's perspective. The goal is to make it easier to find places, ideas, and resources without having to search through countless posts and recommendations.

This website is also a hands-on project where I’m learning and applying web development, responsive design, and SEO while building something useful for people in Karachi.

Why Karachi Gen Z?
